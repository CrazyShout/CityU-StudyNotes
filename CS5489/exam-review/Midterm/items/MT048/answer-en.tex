A feature map can make data linearly separable. The kernel trick replaces mapped inner products by k(x,z), without constructing the mapped vectors. For example, x=\ensuremath{-}1,+1 are positive and x=0 negative. Mapping \ensuremath{\Phi}(x)=x\textsuperscript{2} separates them at 0.5; its kernel is k(x,z)=x\textsuperscript{2}z\textsuperscript{2}.
