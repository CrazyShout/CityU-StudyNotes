Compare primal and dual formulations and use a solver suited to the data. The primal has about 100,000 weights plus a bias; the dual has 2,000 sample multipliers and a 2,000\ensuremath{\times}2,000 Gram matrix. A dual method may therefore be attractive when d\ensuremath{\gg}N. Also exploit sparse inputs and efficient linear-SVM implementations; validated feature reduction is another option. Variable counts alone do not guarantee a wall-clock speedup.
