The first term is L2 regularization; it discourages large weights. The sum is the logistic negative conditional log-likelihood for labels y\textsubscript{i}\ensuremath{\in}\{\ensuremath{-}1,+1\}; it rewards assigning higher probability to the true label. Larger \ensuremath{\alpha} strengthens shrinkage and may underfit; smaller \ensuremath{\alpha} permits larger weights and may overfit. Select \ensuremath{\alpha} by validation. Under a zero-mean isotropic Gaussian weight prior, \ensuremath{\alpha} is proportional to the inverse prior variance.
