\begin{enumerate}[start=1]

\item Give positive/cancer cases or false-negative errors a larger training cost, so missing a positive is penalized more.

\item Lower the threshold for predicting the positive class. If positive is predicted when f(x)>t, lower t; for a calibrated positive-class probability, lower the probability threshold below the usual 0.5 as appropriate.

\end{enumerate}


Choose the threshold on validation data using sensitivity and false-positive cost. These choices favour recall but cannot guarantee zero false negatives on unseen patients.
