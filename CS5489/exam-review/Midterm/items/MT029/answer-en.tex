\textbf{A, D and E} are the intended incorrect statements. Kernel models can also fit linearly separable data, and valid kernels can be defined on strings or sets. Support vectors are identified during fitting; training does not start by knowing which examples can be discarded. B is an informal description: nonzero dual coefficients define predictive support, and points outside the SVR tube can also be support vectors.
