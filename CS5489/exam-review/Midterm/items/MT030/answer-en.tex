The supplied key selects \textbf{D and E}. Read D carefully: both can shrink coefficients, but \textbf{automatic exact-zero selection is characteristic of L1}, not ordinary ridge. E describes a possible generalization benefit, not a guarantee. L2 can improve a least-squares system's conditioning; L1 is nonsmooth and does not provide the same inverse-matrix argument. Weight regularization alone is not a robust residual loss.
