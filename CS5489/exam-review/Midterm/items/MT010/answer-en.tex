The supplied solution expects \textbf{+1 for Gaussian Bayes and \ensuremath{-}1 for quadratic-kernel SVM}. Bayes compares \(p(D\mid c)P(c)\). The more numerous red points give a larger estimated red-class prior, which can offset a density disadvantage at D; the supplied schematic fit favours red. It is not simply a nearest-centre decision. The quadratic SVM can enclose the inner green points with a curved boundary, placing D on their negative side.
